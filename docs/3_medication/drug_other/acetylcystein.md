 
## Acetylcysteine

### Preparation 
Nobstruct 300mg/3ml

### Evidence
Nonacetaminophen-induced hepatic disease: NAC reduced liver biochemistries and serum ferritin in three children with TPN-associated liver disease. Each was begun on estimated maintenance requirements of cysteine (20–50 mg/kg/day of NAC). The NAC dose was increased by 10 mg/kg/day q 1–2 mo to maximum doses of 70, 120, and 135 mg/kg/day. Doses were infused over 12 hours and continued for 3–18 months. The improvements in liver function occurred with larger NAC dosages. A prospective, randomized study including children and adults (1–64 years of age) with early liver toxicity due to allogenic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation reported that the introduction of NAC at the first sign of liver toxicity did not prevent progression of liver disease. A retrospective review including 170 children with nonacetaminophen-induced acute liver failure compared standard of care (n =59) and standard of care plus NAC (n =111). NAC, given as continuous infusion of 100 mg/kg/day for a median of 5 days (range 1–77 days), was safe and associated with shorter length of hospital stay, higher incidence of native liver recovery without transplantation, and better survival after transplantation compared to the historical standard of care. These findings are consistent with the adult literature

### Other indication
- Prevention of acute renal failure associated with nephrotoxic agents
- Prevention of acute kidney injury after cardiopulmonary bypass
- Prevention of bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD)

### General dosing
- Maximum dosage: Cummulative dose of 300 mg/kg per treatment course
- Phía dưới là liều khuyến cáo cho suy gan cấp do acetaminophen, không thấy đề cập liều chính thức do tổn thương gan do các nguyên nhân khác
- Trong thực hành lâm sàng, **chỉ định khi men gan > 500 UI**

- IV: Acetadote: Note: Dosing regimens vary and have not been directly compared; experts do not recommend one regimen over another, but only recommend a regimen that provides a total dose of 300 mg/kg during the first 20 to 24 hours. Administration beyond 20 or 21 hours may be necessary based on clinical scenario if stopping criteria are not met.

**Three-bag method:**
Infants, Children, and Adolescents: Limited data available in patients <5 kg:
21-hour regimen: Consists of 3 doses; total dose delivered: 300 mg/kg; maximum total dose: 30 g
-	Loading dose: IV: 150 mg/kg infused over 60 minutes; maximum dose: 15 g/dose.
-	Second dose: IV: 50 mg/kg infused over 4 hours; maximum dose: 5 g/dose.
-	Third dose: IV: 100 mg/kg infused over 16 hours; maximum dose: 10 g/dose.

**Two-bag method:**
Infants, Children, and Adolescents: Limited data available in patients <41 kg: Note: Although not FDA approved in patients <41 kg, the "two-bag method" is safe and reduces adverse reactions and administration errors.
20-hour regimen: Consists of 2 doses; total dose delivered: 300 mg/kg; maximum total dose: 30 g (Ref).
-	First dose: IV: 200 mg/kg infused over 4 hours; maximum dose: 20 g/dose.
-	Second dose: IV: 100 mg/kg infused over 16 hours; maximum dose: 10 g/dose.

**Phác đồ bệnh viện Nhi đồng 1 (Standard clinical practice)**
- Liều 1: 150 mg/kg/1 giờ
- Liều 2: 50 mg/kg/4 giờ 
- Liều 3: 6.25 mg/kg/**giờ**trong 20 giờ 
- Liều duy trì: 6.25 mg/kg/**giờ**trong 24 giờ

### Contraindications
Caution in patients with asthma or history of bronchospasm. Acute ushing and erythema can occur 30–60 minutes after initiation of the infusion, often with spontaneous resolution despite continued infusion

### Warnings/Precautions
- Infusion related cautions: Anaphylactoid reactions and death have been reported and some recommend infusing the loading dose over 60 minutes

### Administration
- Maximum concentration: Up to 50 mg/mL for loading dose and 10 mg/mL for maintenance dose. Adjust total volume
as needed for patients’ weight and those requiring uid restriction
- Standard clinical practice: **Tại khoa nhiễm, tất cả các liều đều được pha để đạt nồng độ 40mg/ml**
- Intermittent infusion: 
    + Loading dose should be given over 60 minutes to reduce the potential for a life-threatening anaphylactoid reaction
    + First (50 mg/kg) and second (100 mg/kg) maintenance doses should be given over 4 hours and 16 hours
- IV push: Not administered by this method
- Suitable diluents D5W, ½NS, and SW
